    Chicago Real Estate FAQs

    What areas of Chicago does The Shino Group serve?

    We serve Chicago neighborhoods including Lincoln Square, Ravenswood, Bucktown, Avondale, Edgewater, Irving Park, North Center, Uptown, West Town, Roscoe Village, River West, Gold Coast and Ukrainian Village.

    How do I start buying a home in Chicago?

    Start with a consultation to discuss your goals, budget and timeline. We then help with property search, neighborhood information, offer preparation and transaction guidance.

    Can you help me sell my property?

    Yes. Selling support covers pricing strategy, listing preparation, marketing, presentation, offer evaluation and negotiation. We cannot guarantee a sale price.
